## C-V2-ELMINSTER

Dragon Heist PDF 198 names Elminster Warden of Waterdeep. That office is a separate restricted claim.

Adventures PDF 109, in the Shadowdale discussion, says his former windmill has been locked and abandoned for a decade and that Elminster hasn't been seen in all that time. It says it would be foolish to assume he is dead. V2.2 does not add the word “there.” It does not treat the sentence as proof of absence from all Faerûn, or as proof that the absence is limited to Shadowdale. It does not infer death, resignation, a vacant office, a current location, a replacement, or a return date.

Required decision: whether the older wardenship persists.
