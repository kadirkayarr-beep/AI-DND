# Waterdeep source extraction candidates

Status of every record: **UNREVIEWED**. Nothing here is approved canon, a production pack, or an activation.

These records are boundaries for a later review. Inside a reviewed profile, the story model may still propose minor people, conversations, small incidents, ordinary objects, and campaign-local stories. It may not invent an official ruler, rewrite history, or commit world state. Python keeps the Reality Envelope, the Reality Gate, mechanics, and persistence.

Current-authority check: Heroes of Faerûn was image-only and was not cited. Adventures in Faerûn did not supply a Waterdeep chapter in this pass. The facts below are supplemental descriptions from 2015 and 2018 books. Their survival unchanged into 1501 DR is **unknown** unless a record says otherwise.

Printed pages: Dragon Heist contents numbers are used when the heading was found on the following PDF page. Other printed pages say SOURCE NOT VERIFIED.

## Essential current-candidate facts

### WD-F-001

- Normalized fact: Waterdeep is a great port city, called the City of Splendors and the Crown of the North, rising from a deep harbor beside a mountain in the Sea of Swords.
- Source: Waterdeep: Dragon Heist, 2018, introduction; Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide, 2015, Waterdeep.
- Printed page: SOURCE NOT VERIFIED. PDF pages: Dragon Heist 6; Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide 56.
- Section: Introduction; Waterdeep.
- Authority: supplemental setting. Era: 2015 and 2018 descriptions. Class: setting fact. Time: current candidate, 1501 confirmation unknown.
- Knowledge: public.
- Allowed use: orient the city as a northern port metropolis.
- Prohibited: inventing a new capital role or moving the city.
- Evidence: both books name the city, the harbor, and the mountain.
- Conflict: none between these two passages. Review: UNREVIEWED.

### WD-F-002

- Normalized fact: Waterdeep is ruled by an Open Lord who sits openly, together with other lords whose identities are hidden even from each other.
- Source: Waterdeep: Dragon Heist, 2018.
- Printed page: 166, from the contents alignment (PDF 167). PDF page: 167.
- Section: Volo's Waterdeep Enchiridion, history of the lords' rule.
- Authority: supplemental setting. Era: institution described as still in force; origin is historical (1032 DR is a separate record). Class: setting fact. Time: current candidate.
- Knowledge: public that hidden lords exist; their identities are restricted.
- Allowed use: officials, magistrates, and masked judgment as civic color.
- Prohibited: naming a new Open Lord, unmasking a lord, or killing the office.
- Evidence: the enchiridion says Ahghairon decreed this government and that the Open Lord still sits that throne.
- Conflict: none on the form of government. Review: UNREVIEWED.
