## How to review one record

Accept a record only when the printed or PDF page can be checked against the book and the era is explicit. A 2018 description is a candidate for the city as that book knew it. It becomes a 1501 DR fact only when a current-authority page agrees, or when the owner accepts the supplemental book for that point in writing.

Leave restricted facts out of player-facing narration. Leave adventure records out of history.
