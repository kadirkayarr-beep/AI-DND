# Proposed 1501 DR public baseline

This is a proposed reading of the verified public current-candidate facts. It is not approved, not loaded, and not player knowledge. Restricted sentences are omitted. Supplemental records are omitted. Records remain DRAFT and UNREVIEWED.

The current year is 1501 DR, the Year of the Shining Mythal.

Waterdeep is the City of Splendors. Laeral Silverhand is the known Open Lord. Masked Lords rule beside her anonymously. Vajra Safahr holds the separate office of the Blackstaff, and Blackstaff Tower is the city's magical academy. The wards are not divided by walls or gates. The current ward sentence names the Sea, North, Trade, Southern, and Dock Wards, plus the City of the Dead. The Field Ward lies outside that set, without routine city-guard patrol or city services. The Southern Ward has many immigrants. The Dock Ward is where many of the poorest live and work. Defenders named here are the militia, the city guard, eight walking statues the Blackstaff can wake, and the griffon cavalry. Adventurers can descend into the twenty-three-floor Undermountain from the well in the Yawning Portal. Halaster lairs at the bottom. Skullport exists inside Undermountain. The Xanathar Guild is a criminal organization operating from Skullport into Waterdeep. Waterdeep forbids a Talona temple and priesthood, not private lawful reverence. Small shrines to strange gods sometimes appear.

Baldur's Gate stands on the Chionthar, grew from Balduran's foundation, and receives seagoing ships at Gray Harbor. Patriars live in the Upper City, most other people in the Lower City, and refugees and the destitute in the Outer City. Walls and gates divide the three districts. Little Calimshan is a walled immigrant district a short way east. The Council of Four has three dukes and one grand duke, enacts laws, directs the Flaming Fist and the navy, and collects taxes. Ulder Ravengard is grand duke and supreme marshal. The current book also names Duke Dillar Portyr, Duke Brevek Faenor, and Duke Belynne Stelmane. The Parliament of Peers has fifty members, meets in the High Hall, and recommends policy without enacting laws. The Watch keeps the Upper City from the Watch Citadel and clears it at dusk except for residents, designated guests, and authorized servants with a Watch token. The Council pays the Flaming Fist. Its headquarters is the Seatower of Balduran. It defers to the Watch in the Upper City, is most active in the Lower City, rarely patrols the Outer City, and staffs Wyrm's Rock for taxes and the night bridges. The Guild is the principal local criminal syndicate, led by Nine-Fingers Keene. The inner gates are Baldur's Gate, Gond Gate, Heap Gate, Manor Gate, and Sea Gate. The outer gates include Basilisk Gate, Black Dragon Gate, Citadel Gate, and Cliffgate. The road from Black Dragon Gate leads north to Waterdeep. The Dead Three have returned, and their worshipers hide in the city. The city's tolerance lets evil worship, including devil cults, fester. The Unrolling Scroll is an Oghma temple around a reflecting pool. Lady's Hall is the Tymora temple in the Upper City. The Water Queen's House is the oldest temple, devoted to Umberlee, at the end of a stone pier, and closed to outsiders. Waterdeep and Baldur's Gate are in the Lords' Alliance.

Castle Ward, restricted identities, and the Manshoon dossier are not part of this public baseline.
