# V2 conflicts and owner decisions

These conflicts are open. No silent reconciliation was applied. A publication year is not a DR year. Where a current book is silent, the older sentence stays supplemental.

## C-V2-MOON

House of the Moon on Dragon Heist PDF 176, a Selûne tower about seventy-five feet tall, competes with Tower of the Moon on the PDF 177 table. Adventures PDF 45 uses House of the Moon inside an adventure hook. Required decision: which name and site, if either, is the 1501 temple. No destruction or renaming story was invented.

## C-V2-LUCK

Tower of Luck is in the Sea Ward narrative on PDF 176 and in the Castle Ward column of the PDF 177 table. Required decision: which ward, if the 2018 book is adopted at all.

## C-V2-TRADE

Heroes PDF 98 says Trade Ward. Dragon Heist says Trades Ward. Required decision: alias, errata, or two labels. The current sentence is not overwritten.

## C-V2-PORTYR

Adventures PDF 273: Dillar Portyr. Descent into Avernus PDF 163: Dillard Portyr. Required decision: spelling or errata. The current roster still seats this duke beside Ravengard, Faenor, and Stelmane.

## C-V2-COUNCIL

The current council is Ravengard, Dillar Portyr, Brevek Faenor, and Belynne Stelmane. Thalamra Vanthampur's coup is an adventure plot. How she left the earlier portrait is unknown. Required decision: leave her fate unknown.

## C-V2-GATES

Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide PDF 47 limits ordinary Upper-Lower passage to the gate called Baldur's Gate. Adventures PDF 281 calls the four Patriar Gates vital for commerce and travel. Required decision: which access rule applies in 1501.

## C-V2-ELMINSTER

Dragon Heist PDF 198 names Elminster Warden of Waterdeep under Laeral. Adventures PDF 109 says he has not been seen at his Shadowdale tower for a decade and warns against assuming he is dead. Required decision: whether the wardenship persists. Absence in Shadowdale is not a resignation.

## C-V2-UNDER

The 2015 ban on unauthorized living or digging below Waterdeep is a different act from descending the existing Yawning Portal well, which Heroes PDF 98 confirms. Required decision: whether the 2015 ban still has force in 1501. No license was invented for Durnan.

## C-V2-EPOCH

Supplemental books from 2015, 2018, and 2019 describe their own portraits. Dragon Heist did not print a DR year on the pages used. Avernus dates its gazetteer to 1492 DR. Heroes dates the present to 1501 DR. Required decision, claim by claim: which supplemental sentences are adopted for 1501. A blanket adoption is not requested.

## C-V2-SKULLPORT-STATE

Heroes PDF 98 calls Skullport bustling. Dungeon of the Mad Mage PDF 305 says most businesses are shut. Required decision: the shutdown stays a module snapshot unless a current page adopts it. Routes on PDF 304 stay discovery-required.

WD-H-003 is metadata plus an in-world out-of-print book, not one historical fact. WD-H-004 is a 2018 Undercliff description, not history. WD-A-005 is DM advice. WD-A-001, WD-A-002, WD-A-003's Kolat scenario, BG-A-002, BG-A-003, and BG-A-004 remain source-supported possibilities outside established history.
