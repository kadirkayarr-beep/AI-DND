# Owner decision: Castle Ward in 1501 DR

Date: 8 October 2026.

Decision class: OWNER-APPROVED CONTINUITY DECISION.

This is not a direct 2025 source verification and is not an official 2025 source fact.

Status: recorded for a later controlled revision. Not loaded. Not granted to any character. Not a campaign event. DRAFT and UNREVIEWED remain in force for `WD22-F-015` and the existing freeze candidate.

## Approved statement

Castle Ward continues to exist as a ward of Waterdeep in 1501 DR, the Year of the Shining Mythal.

The project owner explicitly approved this continuity on 8 October 2026.

## Exact scope

The approval establishes only that Castle Ward still exists as one of Waterdeep's wards in 1501 DR.

It does not establish that every 2015 or 2018 official, building, temple, security arrangement, or historical condition in that ward remains unchanged.

## Related record

`WD22-F-015`.

Heroes of Faerûn PDF 98 names some wards with the word "include" and does not name Castle Ward. The freeze candidate included that printed list and excluded the inference that the omission abolishes Castle Ward. This decision supplies the missing continuity choice. It does not rewrite `WD22-F-015`, its errata, or the 95-record freeze manifests.

## Sources

- Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide (2015), PDF 55, describes Castle Ward, including Castle Waterdeep and Piergeiron's Palace.
- Waterdeep: Dragon Heist (2018), PDF 7, 171, and 177, treats Castle Ward as a living ward and places Ahghairon's Tower and named temples there.
- Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn (2025), PDF 98, gives a non-exhaustive ward list and does not mention Castle Ward.
- Forgotten Realms: Adventures in Faerûn (2025) did not, in the inspected text, confirm or abolish Castle Ward.

## Evidentiary limit

No inspected 2025 page states that Castle Ward exists in 1501 DR, and none states that it was removed. Older presence is not itself 1501 proof. The continuity therefore rests on this owner approval.

If a later current official source contradicts this decision, the conflict is flagged for owner review. Neither the 2025 text nor this decision is silently overridden.

## Effect

The approved sentence may be proposed in a future revised freeze. It is not part of the current 95-record candidate until that revision is written and reviewed. Python remains the authority for what any character knows and for what Qwen may narrate. Public classification would still not grant the fact to every character.
