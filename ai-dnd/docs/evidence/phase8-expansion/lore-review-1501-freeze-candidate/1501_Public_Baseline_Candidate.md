## Waterdeep

The current year is 1501 DR, the Year of the Shining Mythal (`FR22-F-001`, Heroes PDF 59).

Waterdeep is the City of Splendors (`WD22-F-001`, `WD22-E-001`, Heroes PDF 98). Laeral Silverhand is the known Open Lord (`WD22-F-003`, `WD22-F-005`, `WD22-E-002`, `WD22-E-003`, `WD22-X-002`). Masked Lords rule beside her anonymously (`WD22-E-004`, `WD22-X-003-1`). Vajra Safahr holds the Blackstaff, an office distinct from the Open Lordship, and Blackstaff Tower is the magical academy (`WD22-F-054`, `WD22-E-017`, `WD22-X-009`).

The wards named on Heroes PDF 98 are the Sea Ward, North Ward, Trade Ward, Southern Ward, Dock Ward, and the City of the Dead. The Field Ward is outside that set (`WD22-F-014`, `WD22-F-015` included clause only, `WD22-F-016`, `WD22-F-017-2`, `WD22-F-048`, `WD22-F-050`, `WD22-E-015`, `WD22-E-016`, `WD22-X-008`). Castle Ward is not part of this candidate. The Southern Ward has many immigrants. Many of the poorest live and work in the Dock Ward. The Field Ward has no routine city-guard patrol and no city sanitation services.

Named defenders are a militia, a city guard, eight walking statues the Blackstaff can wake, and the griffon cavalry. This page does not restate a Watch-versus-Guard beat (`WD22-F-008`, `WD22-F-010`, `WD22-F-045`, `WD22-E-006`, `WD22-E-007`).

Undermountain is a twenty-three-floor megadungeon. The Yawning Portal well is an entrance. Halaster lairs at the bottom level; his motives are unknown, and he built and stocked the dungeon. Other creatures founded places there, including Skullport (`WD22-F-041`, `WD22-F-058-1`, `WD22-F-058-2`, `WD22-F-058-3`, `WD22-F-058-4`, `WD22-F-066`, `WD22-E-011`, `WD22-E-013`, `WD22-E-014`, `WD22-X-006`). The Xanathar Guild is a criminal organization operating from Skullport into Waterdeep (`WD22-F-065`, `WD22-E-020`). Its leader's identity is not in this public candidate.

Waterdeep forbids a Talona temple and priesthood. Private reverence while obeying the law is not punished (`WD22-F-026`, Heroes PDF 120). Small shrines to strange gods sometimes appear (`WD22-F-027`, Heroes PDF 117).

## Baldur's Gate

Baldur's Gate stands on the Chionthar, grew from Balduran's foundation, and receives seagoing ships at Gray Harbor (`BG22-F-001`, `BG22-E-001`, `BG22-E-005`, `BG22-E-006`, `BG22-X-007`, Adventures PDF 270 and 288). Patriars live in the Upper City, most other people in the Lower City, and refugees and the destitute in the Outer City. Walls and gates divide the three districts (`BG22-F-002`, `BG22-E-002`, `BG22-E-003`, `BG22-E-004`, Heroes PDF 97 and Adventures PDF 270). Little Calimshan is a walled immigrant district a short way east (`BG22-F-003`, `BG22-E-019`).

The Council of Four has three dukes and one grand duke. It enacts laws, directs the Flaming Fist and the navy, and collects taxes (`BG22-F-004`, `BG22-E-007`, `BG22-X-002`). Ulder Ravengard is grand duke and supreme marshal. The cited pages do not place him in a scene (`BG22-F-005`, `BG22-E-012`, `BG22-X-001`, Adventures PDF 273 and 274). Adventures PDF 273 also names Duke Dillar Portyr, Duke Brevek Faenor, and Duke Belynne Stelmane (`BG22-F-006`, `BG22-E-013`, `BG22-E-014`). That spelling does not merge Dillar with Dillard. Stelmane's separate restricted record is not repeated here.

The Parliament of Peers has fifty members, meets in the High Hall, and recommends policy without enacting laws (`BG22-F-009`, `BG22-E-008`, `BG22-E-015`, `BG22-X-003`). The Watch is paid by the patriars, based in the Watch Citadel, and keeps the Upper City. At dusk it clears anyone who is not a resident, a designated guest, or an authorized servant with a Watch token (`BG22-F-010`, `BG22-E-009`, `BG22-X-004`, `BG22-X-009`).

The Council pays the Flaming Fist to defend, patrol, and punish (`BG22-F-011-1`). Headquarters is the Seatower of Balduran (`BG22-F-011-2`). The Fist defers to the Watch in the Upper City (`BG22-F-011-3`), is most active in the Lower City (`BG22-F-011-4`), rarely patrols the Outer City (`BG22-F-011-5`), and staffs Wyrm's Rock for taxes and the night bridges (`BG22-F-011-6`, `BG22-E-010`, `BG22-E-016`, `BG22-X-005`, `BG22-X-010`).

The Guild is the principal local criminal syndicate, led by Nine-Fingers Keene, with the lighter-touch direction stated on Adventures PDF 277 (`BG22-F-012`, `BG22-F-013-1`, `BG22-F-013-2`, `BG22-E-011`, `BG22-E-018`, `BG22-X-008`). The separate restricted Guild record is omitted.

Inner gates are Baldur's Gate, Gond Gate, Heap Gate, Manor Gate, and Sea Gate. Outer gates include Basilisk Gate, Black Dragon Gate, Citadel Gate, and Cliffgate. The road from Black Dragon Gate leads north to Waterdeep (`BG22-F-015`, `BG22-X-006`, Adventures PDF 281). Patriar manors are opulent Upper City houses, and one house's secret is not every house's secret (`BG22-F-016`, `BG22-E-017`).

The Dead Three have returned, and their worshipers hide in the city (`BG22-F-018`, Heroes PDF 97). The city's tolerance lets evil worship, including devil cults, fester (`BG22-F-019`). The Unrolling Scroll is an Oghma temple around a reflecting pool (`BG22-F-021`). Lady's Hall is the Tymora temple in the Upper City (`BG22-F-022`). The Water Queen's House is the oldest temple, devoted to Umberlee, at the end of a stone pier, and closed to outsiders (`BG22-F-023`). The separate restricted temple record is omitted.

Waterdeep and Baldur's Gate are members of the Lords' Alliance (`BG22-F-027`, Heroes PDF 97).
