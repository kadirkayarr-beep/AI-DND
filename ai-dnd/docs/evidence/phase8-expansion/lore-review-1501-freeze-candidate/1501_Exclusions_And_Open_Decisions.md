# Exclusions and open decisions

The public candidate contains 95 records: 94 full and `WD22-F-015` in part. Five restricted records are listed separately. 102 other V2.2 records stay out of unconditional 1501 use. None of these exclusions is an approval decision.

## Partial record

`WD22-F-015` is included only for the ward names printed on Heroes PDF 98: Sea Ward, North Ward, Trade Ward, Southern Ward, Dock Ward, the City of the Dead, and the Field Ward outside them. The wording on the page is "include."

Castle Ward is not named there. The sentence that this absence does not abolish Castle Ward is excluded. That is missing evidence for a 1501 Castle Ward statement. It is not a second source contradicting Heroes PDF 98.

## Restricted records, not public lore

`WD22-F-067`, `WD22-F-068`, `BG22-F-007`, `BG22-F-013-3`, and `BG22-F-024` match their cited pages and remain restricted. This candidate grants neither public narration nor player knowledge.

`WD22-F-068` states a failed coup and a lurking Manshoon clone in an Adventures PDF 393 NPC dossier. That text is not an automatically played campaign event. Using it in a campaign is an owner decision.

## Spelling and identity decisions

Adventures PDF 273 names Duke Dillar Portyr, and that public sentence is in the candidate through `BG22-F-006`. Descent into Avernus PDF 163 spells Dillard Portyr. `BG22-C-002` and `BG22-E-020` stay excluded. The two spellings are a genuine source difference under `C-V2-PORTYR`. This candidate does not merge them and does not declare either spelling an erratum.

Other open source differences, also excluded from this candidate:

- `C-V2-TRADE`: Trade Ward on Heroes PDF 98 and Trades Ward in Dragon Heist. The candidate uses the Heroes spelling because that is the current-source sentence. It does not create an alias.
- `C-V2-MOON`: House of the Moon and Tower of the Moon.
- `C-V2-LUCK`: Tower of Luck's ward.
- `C-V2-GATES`: the 2015 ordinary-traffic limit and the current vital-gates sentence.
- `C-V2-ELMINSTER`: the Warden sentence and the Shadowdale absence sentence. Neither is adopted as death, resignation, or a vacant office.

These are competing or differently scoped source statements. They are not blank pages.

## Supplemental and other non-current records

68 records remain `supplemental_unconfirmed_1501`. They are excluded from unconditional present-day use. Their 1501 continuity is not established. That is one contained epoch question, `C-V2-EPOCH`, not 68 missing pages and not a contradiction of Heroes PDF 59.

Also excluded:

- 13 `not_1501_fact` records, including adventure possibilities and rejected claims.
- 10 `historical_setting` records. They can remain history without becoming a current scene.
- 10 `conflict_open` records, listed through the decisions above.
- 1 `not_established_1501` record, `WD22-F-069`, the Mad Mage shutdown portrait of Skullport. Heroes PDF 98 describes Skullport as existing. The shutdown is not established for 1501. That is a module snapshot kept out of the candidate, not a contradiction inside the Heroes sentence.

## What further evidence would change

Further evidence is required only where the current pages do not state the claim: Castle Ward in 1501, present-day force for a supplemental sentence, and whether the owner adopts the Manshoon dossier as this campaign's plot. The Portyr, Trade, Moon, Luck, Gates, and Elminster items need an owner choice between existing statements, not another search for a sentence that was already found.
