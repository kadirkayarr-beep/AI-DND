## Knowledge and authority

The staff bypass and Aurinax's custody stay restricted and are absent from the public edge. Banking-network agents are restricted even though Nine-Fingers' leadership is public. Xanathar Guild's existence is public. Xanathar's nature, Sylgar, and the rivalry sentence that follows the identity reveal are restricted.

Qwen may narrate only a result Python has already resolved. These rows do not move characters, open gates, resolve the dragonward, change factions, or roll dice. They also do not forbid a future event that Python accepts and records.

Player knowledge is not granted by a public lore label. Python and the campaign record decide what a character has learned.
