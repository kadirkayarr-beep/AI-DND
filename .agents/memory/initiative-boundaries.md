---
name: Initiative boundaries
description: Why membership changes must preserve progress and defer death/end decisions to their owners.
---

New participants placed before the current actor wait until the next round;
those after it may act this round. Do not reinterpret joining as a fresh encounter.

**Why:** Resetting existing actors or replaying already-passed turns would grant
extra actions and break the owner's existing Action Economy and time boundaries.

**How to apply:** Preserve the current actor, completed progress and existing
budgets when implementing future summoning, reinforcements or relocation.

Slot eligibility is not the combat-ending criterion. Unconscious slots remain
available to the Death owner during an active encounter; the existing lifecycle
still decides when no opponents can fight.

**Why:** Using retained unconscious NPC slots as evidence of a fighting opponent
would prevent the established verified-hit end behavior. Conversely, blindly
ending when the final opponent leaves would discard an unconscious player's
death checkpoint.

**How to apply:** Route final departures and death-resolution boundaries through
Combat/Death rather than deciding defeat or recovery inside Initiative.
