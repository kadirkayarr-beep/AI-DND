---
name: Spell scope boundaries
description: Owner-approved bounded spell scope and reasons not to infer preparation, actor control or deferred mechanics.
---

Spell scope is intentionally limited to Fire Bolt, Magic Missile, Cure Wounds
and Sacred Flame modes that existing authoritative owners can actually resolve.
Do not expand the catalog, class progression, concentration, NPC control or spell
UI merely to make the framework appear complete. Unsupported modes must reject
before action/slot/RNG/time consumption, not become invented canonical effects.

**Why:** The user explicitly scoped Spell Scope Lock to one bounded pass and
reserved UI presentation and later mechanics for separate ownership.

**How to apply:** Treat a spellbook selection as distinct from preparation;
source-incompatible or absent preparation/control owners justify unavailable
modes, not inferred grants. Preserve locked Time/Action/Attack/Death/receipt owners.

Native spell damage proof and API replay proof remain separate from an external
actor route that does not exist. Do not fabricate NPC healing/casting to certify
zero-HP player recipient behavior.

**Why:** The production action route controls the player, and the user prohibits
inventing missing actor architecture; an unconscious player cannot self-cast.

**How to apply:** Report the exact reachable recipient mode, retaining engine
healing authority without claiming an external caster is production-connected.

Listed spell access/preparation is not permission for a current cast.

**Why:** Creation can record spells whose resolution modes are deferred, and
current legality also depends on authoritative targets, resources and turn state.

**How to apply:** Present the supplied lists and remaining resources read-only.
Do not turn a listed spell into an enabled cast control based only on its presence,
or fill an empty prepared list from a spellbook.
