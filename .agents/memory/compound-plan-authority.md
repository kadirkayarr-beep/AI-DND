---
name: Compound plan authority
description: Why clear compound intentions are source-bound in Python before physical routing.
---

For clearly enumerated first-person compound intentions, prefer exact player
source spans over asking the model to recreate canonical steps. Recognizing a
plan must precede scene materialization and non-combat action interpretation,
not just ignore their returned result after they have already changed the world.

**Why:** Real Qwen plan interpretation remained unreliable despite a required,
nonempty steps schema. Cross-clause matching also combined a room-search verb
with a conditional merchant cover story and incorrectly discovered a merchant.
The owner required no new classifier call, retry, execution or time/RNG advance.

**How to apply:** Keep source extraction bounded and quoted speech opaque. Keep
strict grounding, step limits, earlier-only dependencies and unresolved
conditions. Planning alone never authorizes a spell, search or social claim;
active combat, death and explicit structured commands retain their owners.
Use the existing receipt/save path and treat any optional model output only as
post-resolution narration. Do not equate this bounded grammar with full Turkish
language coverage or automatic condition evaluation.
