======================================================================
THE FOUR REALITY LAYERS
======================================================================

Implement an explicit distinction between:

1. OFFICIAL SETTING CANON
2. CAMPAIGN CANON
3. PROCEDURAL LOCAL FICTION
4. NARRATIVE TEXTURE

These are NOT interchangeable.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. OFFICIAL SETTING CANON
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Facts originating from approved official Forgotten Realms sources.

Examples:

- Toril
- Faerûn
- Sword Coast
- Waterdeep
- Neverwinter
- Baldur's Gate
- canonical geography
- canonical gods
- canonical factions
- canonical cosmology
- important named landmarks
- important official NPCs
- established cultures
- established historical events

These facts require provenance.

Qwen does not create them.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CAMPAIGN CANON
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Facts established by THIS player's authoritative campaign after play begins.

Example:

Official baseline:
NPC X is alive.

Campaign event:
player canonically defeats NPC X.

Campaign canon now records the outcome.

The Lore Authority must NOT overwrite the player's campaign reality merely
because the published setting originally had NPC X alive.

Official canon defines the baseline.

Campaign events may create a branch afterward.

This preserves player agency.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PROCEDURAL LOCAL FICTION
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Small-scale campaign-created content that fills gaps without contradicting
official canon.

Examples MAY include:

- an ordinary local baker
- an unnamed stablehand
- a minor farmer
- a procedural small house
- an incidental traveler
- a small local problem
- a non-famous minor business if no canon conflict exists

Procedural content MUST be labeled internally as procedural.

It must NEVER be presented internally as official Forgotten Realms canon.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
4. NARRATIVE TEXTURE
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Temporary descriptive details:

- candlelight
- street noise
- rain on slate
- smoke
- distant carts
- ordinary crowds
- tavern smells
- birds
- mundane decoration

These are NOT automatically persisted as world facts.

Narrative texture remains the Narrative Director's freedom.

======================================================================

======================================================================
SOURCE TIER 1 — CURRENT CORE FORGOTTEN REALMS
======================================================================

Prefer current official Forgotten Realms setting material where available.

The project should recognize as high-priority current official sources:

- Forgotten Realms: Adventures in Faerûn
- Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn

Use them only if source content is legally/technically available to the

Do not scrape or reproduce inaccessible paid sourcebook text.

======================================================================
SOURCE TIER 2 — OFFICIAL 5E FORGOTTEN REALMS MATERIAL
======================================================================

Includes sources such as:

- Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide

======================================================================
SOURCE TIER 3 — OLDER OFFICIAL REALMS MATERIAL
======================================================================

Older-edition official Forgotten Realms material may be used ONLY when:

- current sources do not supersede it
- no contradiction exists
- temporal context is understood

Label it internally as:

LEGACY_OFFICIAL

Do not silently treat a 2e/3e fact as current-era reality.

======================================================================
SOURCE TIER 4 — OFFICIAL FICTION / GAMES
======================================================================

Official novels and games may provide useful supplemental context.

However:

they must not automatically override current tabletop setting sources.

Treat them as supplementary unless corroborated or explicitly selected by
the owner as authoritative for a campaign.

Do not silently use video-game-only events as tabletop canon.

======================================================================
SOURCE TIER 5 — PROCEDURAL CAMPAIGN CONTENT
======================================================================

Lowest authority.

Procedural content can fill gaps.

It can NEVER override official canon.
