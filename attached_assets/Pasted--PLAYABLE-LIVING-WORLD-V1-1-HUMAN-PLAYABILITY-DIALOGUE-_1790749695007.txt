# PLAYABLE LIVING WORLD V1.1
# HUMAN PLAYABILITY / DIALOGUE & INTERACTION HOTFIX

Current protected baseline:

AI-DND v0.6.0-alpha
Internal Build V14

Playable Living World V1 passed its automated release gate, but a real human
manual play session exposed multiple ordinary-play failures.

DO NOT START SPELLS.

This is one coherent hotfix phase intended to make normal Turkish gameplay
actually conversational and playable.

Do not solve these as disconnected keyword patches.

==================================================
PRIMARY GOAL
==================================================

A player must be able to:

observe a city,
approach an ordinary local,
ask their name,
ask their profession,
ask how their day is going,
ask what they did today,
introduce themselves,
ask about a nearby tavern,
enter it,
talk to the bartender,
talk to a traveler,
ask where the traveler came from,
sit at a table,
inspect a scene detail,
ask about ordinary local work,
leave,
return,
ask about merchants,
ask a Forgotten Realms question,
reject unsupported canon safely,
and then immediately continue ordinary play

without:

- debug language
- empty no-op responses
- canon validation being applied to personal local fiction
- lost conversation targets
- false NPC memory
- invented player speech
- unsupported quest promises
- repeated robotic fallback prose

==================================================
1. SEPARATE SETTING LORE FROM LOCAL PERSONAL REALITY
==================================================

The largest remaining product error is applying Forgotten Realms lore
verification to ordinary campaign-local personal facts.

Introduce or formalize the distinction between:

VERIFIED_SETTING_FACT
CAMPAIGN_FACT
PROCEDURAL_PERSONAL_FACT
EPHEMERAL_SCENE_TEXTURE

Examples:

"Who rules Neverwinter?"
= VERIFIED_SETTING_FACT

"What does this local resident do for work?"
= PROCEDURAL_PERSONAL_FACT

"What did this bartender do this morning?"
= PROCEDURAL_PERSONAL_FACT

"Where is this traveler coming from?"
= PROCEDURAL_PERSONAL_FACT, provided it does not invent unsupported official
geography/history.

"Two patrons are talking near the fire."
= EPHEMERAL_SCENE_TEXTURE

Do NOT require Forgotten Realms source provenance for an ordinary procedural
NPC's profession, mood, immediate activity, recent activity or personal
background.

Do NOT allow personal fiction to create official Forgotten Realms facts.

==================================================
2. PROCEDURAL NPC MICRO-PROFILES
==================================================

Persistent procedural NPCs need a small Python-owned local profile sufficient
for natural conversation.

Use bounded fields appropriate to the existing architecture, such as:

- stable ID
- name
- role / occupation
- current activity
- mood or conversational disposition
- recent mundane activity
- reason for being here
- small personal/local detail
- optional local concern or immediate goal
- current location
- relationship / interaction state

Do not generate large biographies.

Fields may be created at initial materialization or lazily when first needed.

Once accepted, a procedural personal fact becomes campaign-local reality and
must remain consistent.

It must NEVER become official Forgotten Realms canon.

Example:

Player:
"What did you do today?"

BAD:
"I don't have verified information about that."

GOOD:
The NPC answers from an accepted campaign-local personal fact.

If this personal fact did not previously exist, the system may generate and
validate one bounded mundane fact, store it, then narrate it.

==================================================
3. CONVERSATION TARGET CONTINUITY
==================================================

Once the player approaches or begins talking to a specific NPC, short
follow-up utterances must resolve to that conversation target.

Examples:

"Adın ne?"
"Mesleğin ne?"
"Bugün ne yaptın?"
"Nereden geliyorsun?"
"Peki sen?"
"Ona dönüyorum."
"Adını sor."

must not lose their NPC target merely because the player's sentence omits the
name.

Use structured active conversation target state.

Do not rely only on keyword matching.

Clear scene movement or explicit target changes may change the active target.

==================================================
4. PLAYER NAME DISCLOSURE
==================================================

Explicit disclosures must work naturally.

Recognize semantically equivalent Turkish forms such as:

"Benim adım Rhea."
"İsmim Rhea."
"Ben Rhea."
"Kendimi tanıtıyorum, adım Rhea."
"Adımı söylüyorum: Rhea."

Bind the disclosure ONLY to the currently addressed NPC(s) that actually
heard it.

Do not require the player to use a special implementation phrase.

A different NPC who never received the disclosure must not know the name
merely because the engine knows the player character's name.

Important distinction:

ENGINE KNOWLEDGE:
the player's character is Rhea

NPC KNOWLEDGE:
Duru knows the player's name only if Rhea disclosed it to Duru or another
validated information-transfer event occurred.

If the player asks Duru:

"Benim adımı biliyor musun?"

and Duru was never told, Duru must not use global character identity to fake
NPC memory.

==================================================
5. ORDINARY DIALOGUE MUST NOT BECOME NO-OP
==================================================

Remove normal conversation from generic no-state-change handling.

A conversation turn can be meaningful even when no durable mechanical state
changes.

State mutation is NOT required for meaningful narration.

Questions such as:

"Bugün nasılsın?"
"Mesleğin ne?"
"Bugün ne yaptın?"
"Nereden geliyorsun?"
"İşler nasıl?"

must receive contextual dialogue.

Do NOT answer them with:

"Belirttiğin eylem dışında sahnede yeni bir değişiklik olmaz."

==================================================
6. REMOVE TECHNICAL PLAYER-FACING FALLBACKS
==================================================

Normal gameplay must NEVER expose text such as:

"Bu eylem için henüz bir Python kuralı tanımlı değil."

"Python world state..."

"kayıtlı NPC..."

"UNVERIFIED..."

"dünya durumu değişmedi"

"Belirttiğin eylem dışında sahnede yeni bir değişiklik olmaz."

or equivalent implementation language.

Search ALL player-facing fallback paths, including deterministic/direct
responses, not only Qwen narration.

Internal diagnostics may retain reason codes.

Every normal unsupported action should receive honest natural DM prose.

==================================================
7. OBSERVATION IS NOT A LORE CLAIM
==================================================

Statements such as:

"şehirde dolanıyorum, etrafımda neler var gözlemliyorum"

must be recognized as observation/exploration.

Do not interpret first-person observation requests as factual Forgotten Realms
claims merely because they mention a canonical city context.

Observation should return:

- current accepted scene entities
- usable affordances
- bounded scene activity
- relevant verified setting texture when available
- safe local procedural texture

==================================================
8. VENUE / LOCAL SEARCH INTENT
==================================================

Correct semantic intent classification.

Example:

"Yakınlarda oturup bir şeyler içebileceğim bir yer var mı?"

means:

find a suitable local establishment

It does NOT mean:

find a chair I can sit on.

Likewise:

"Buralarda bir tüccar var mı?"

is ordinary local search.

It must not automatically become an unsupported lore claim.

Use scene/location semantics, not isolated words such as "oturmak".

==================================================
9. NATURAL LOCAL MERCHANT / ESTABLISHMENT DISCOVERY
==================================================

Within the existing bounded Living World rules, ordinary city exploration may
surface campaign-local procedural:

- inns
- taverns
- merchants
- craftspeople
- guards
- travelers
- residents

without requiring sourcebook provenance.

They remain PROCEDURAL_LOCAL_FICTION.

Do not add economy, prices, inventory commerce or transactions in this hotfix.

==================================================
10. QUERY SUBJECT RESOLUTION
==================================================

Fix the class of bug where a world question is incorrectly interpreted as
asking the active NPC's identity.

Manual example:

Player is talking to Duru.

Player:
"bu şehrin kralı kim?"
"kral kim?"

BAD:
Duru says:
"Ben Duru, Muhafız."

Extract the SUBJECT of the question separately from the conversational
SPEAKER.

The active NPC may answer the question in dialogue if:

- the player clearly asked that NPC,
- the information is public/appropriate for the NPC,
- and Lore Authority has verified support.

Otherwise narrator/world knowledge may answer through the existing grounded
path.

Do not confuse:

"Sen kimsin?"

with:

"Kral kim?"

==================================================
11. NPC SPEECH VS SETTING FACT
==================================================

NPCs may speak about:

- themselves
- their own activity
- local mundane opinions
- personal plans
- ordinary observations

without every sentence requiring lore provenance.

However:

NPC speech does NOT launder unsupported canon.

Example:

BAD:
A procedural guard invents an unsupported official king, exact historical
route, deity or city fact.

Such setting-level claims still require verified lore support.

==================================================
12. DO NOT PROMISE MECHANICS THAT DO NOT EXIST
==================================================

The manual test exposed:

Guard:
"If you're ready to start work, come with me."

Player:
"I'm ready."

Then:

"Python has no rule for this."

This must not happen.

Narration/NPC dialogue may not make a concrete actionable promise that the
engine cannot represent.

Do not create:

- unsupported quests
- promised rewards
- employment systems
- payments
- follow-me sequences
- scripted jobs

unless backed by accepted structured state.

Ordinary local conversation about possible work is fine.

Example:

"There is often day work near the docks."

is local flavor.

"I have a job for you. Follow me now."

requires an actual supported structured opportunity/quest/action.

Do NOT build a new quest or economy system in this hotfix.

==================================================
13. PLAYER AGENCY — ZERO INVENTED PLAYER DIALOGUE
==================================================

The manual test produced narration equivalent to:

'"Can you help me?" you ask.'

when the player never said that.

This is unacceptable.

Qwen must never invent:

- player speech
- player decisions
- player beliefs
- player emotions as fact
- player objectives
- player acceptance
- player movement not requested

Strengthen the Turkish agency guard.

Detect narration structures such as:

"... diye soruyorsun"
"... diyorsun"
"... kabul ediyorsun"
"... karar veriyorsun"

when that speech/action was not actually supplied by the player.

Do not solve this by making narration sterile.
NPCs may act and speak freely within accepted state.

==================================================
14. SKILL CHECK RESULTS MUST BE CONCRETE
==================================================

The manual test produced a critical Investigation success with:

"You understand how the details relate to each other better."

This is mechanically valid but useless.

When Perception/Investigation targets an accepted scene entity or feature,
a successful roll must return concrete bounded observations about THAT target.

Critical/high success should provide additional useful detail when available.

It may reveal:

- visible wear
- demeanor
- carried mundane objects already permitted by scene logic
- signs of recent activity
- physical relationships between accepted scene elements

It must not manufacture:

- secret lore
- inventory ownership
- canonical history
- unsupported route information
- quest clues that do not exist

If there is genuinely nothing additional to discover, say that naturally and
do not pretend a critical success revealed knowledge.

==================================================
15. ARRIVAL / RE-ENTRY MUST FEEL LIKE ARRIVAL
==================================================

Manual example:

"Emre Hanı'na tekrar giriyorum."

response:
"Emre Hanı konumuna ulaştın."

then:

"içeri giriyorum"

response:
generic no-op

Fix this state boundary.

Entering a location should atomically establish:

- current local position
- inside/outside state where relevant
- known/reused scene identity
- arrival narration

The player should not need an extra "içeri giriyorum" after an action that
already meant entering.

If the player says "içeri giriyorum" while already inside, respond naturally:

"Zaten hanın içindesin..."

and continue with current scene context.

Do not use a generic no-op.

==================================================
16. REVISIT NARRATION
==================================================

Returning to a known establishment should reuse the SAME accepted:

- location
- persistent NPCs
- significant features

while allowing bounded ambient variation.

Do not merely say:

"You reached the location."

Provide a short natural arrival beat.

==================================================
17. PROCEDURAL NAMING QUALITY
==================================================

Turkish is the narration language.

That does NOT mean procedural Forgotten Realms residents should automatically
receive modern Turkish names.

Use setting-appropriate procedural fantasy naming for campaign-local NPCs and
establishments.

These names are NOT official canon.

Do not copy canonical characters.

Avoid modern immersion-breaking naming patterns unless justified by the
campaign.

Keep naming bounded and deterministic once accepted.

==================================================
18. TURKISH NARRATION QUALITY
==================================================

Improve the narrator contract for natural Turkish.

Avoid malformed/artificial prose such as:

"genellenebilir bir sessizlik"
"alnından geçen bir gülümseme"
"bir zarif gülümseme"

Prefer concise, concrete DM narration.

Do not require every response to be literary.

Dialogue turns should usually be shorter than scene-establishing turns.

Prefer:

NPC action + spoken response + optional useful scene beat

over long generic paragraphs.

Do not invent decorative randomness merely to sound alive.

==================================================
19. UNKNOWN / UNSUPPORTED LORE RESPONSE QUALITY
==================================================

Unsupported official lore must still be rejected.

But player-facing wording should remain natural.

Avoid repeatedly saying:

"Bu konuda güvenilir bir doğrulamam yok; bilinmeyeni gerçekmiş gibi
aktarmayacağım."

This sounds like an AI fact-checker, not a Dungeon Master.

Use context-sensitive in-world/narrator wording.

Example concept:

"Bu kadar kesin bir mesafeyi doğrulayacak bir bilgin yok."

or an NPC may simply admit they do not know.

Preserve the internal UNVERIFIED reason code only in diagnostics.

==================================================
20. CANON REFUSAL MUST NOT POISON THE NEXT TURN
==================================================

Maintain the successful recovery property demonstrated in manual testing.

After rejecting:

"Kral Vharion şu an sarayında mı?"

or an unsupported exact route/distance,

the next normal action:

"Peki, etrafıma bakıyorum."

must immediately return to ordinary living-world play.
