6. ATTACK RESOLUTION PIPELINE
==================================================

The canonical attack pipeline should conceptually be:

1. Validate current actor / legal attack context.
2. Validate target.
3. Validate weapon or Unarmed Strike.
4. Validate range/reach.
5. Validate visibility/targetability.
6. Determine Cover.
7. Determine Advantage/Disadvantage sources.
8. Determine attack ability.
9. Determine proficiency.
10. Determine attack bonuses/penalties.
11. Resolve authoritative d20.
12. Determine natural 20 / natural 1.
13. Determine Hit / Miss / Critical Hit.
14. If Miss:
      resolve miss-triggered effects such as Graze if applicable.
15. If Hit:
      resolve damage.
16. Apply Critical Hit damage rules if applicable.
17. Apply damage modifiers.
18. Apply Resistance.
19. Apply Vulnerability.
20. Apply Immunity.
21. Apply resulting damage to canonical HP.
22. Resolve weapon mastery effects.
23. Resolve authorized attack feature hooks.
24. Resolve resulting conditions/effects.
25. Hand off any 0 HP / death-related state to the existing Death System.
26. Persist the complete canonical result.
27. Return a verified mechanical result.
28. Only then allow narration.

Do not allow Qwen or frontend to insert steps into this pipeline.

==================================================

Default weapon attack rules:

Strength modifier.

Ranged weapon attack:

Finesse:

Other authoritative features may override the normal ability.

The system must not assume:

or

The same selected ability modifier is used for the weapon damage roll

==================================================
15. FINESSE
==================================================

If the weapon has Finesse:

the attacker may choose Strength or Dexterity.

The chosen modifier must be used consistently for:

- attack roll
- damage roll
- relevant mastery DCs
- other mechanics explicitly based on the ability used for the attack.

Do not let the player use Dexterity for the attack and Strength for

==================================================
16. THROWN WEAPONS
==================================================

If a weapon has Thrown:

it can make a ranged attack.

The weapon may be drawn as part of the attack according to the weapon
rules.

If the Thrown weapon is a Melee weapon:

use the same ability modifier for the ranged thrown attack that would be
used for a melee attack with that weapon.

Finesse still applies if the weapon has Finesse.

Do not incorrectly force all thrown attacks to Dexterity.

==================================================
17. TWO-HANDED
==================================================

A Two-Handed weapon requires two hands when making the attack.

The attack must fail safely if the authoritative equipment/hand state
does not satisfy this requirement and no exception applies.

Do not infer free hands from player prose.

Use canonical equipment state.

==================================================
18. VERSATILE
==================================================

A Versatile weapon can be used one-handed or two-handed.

When used with two hands:

use the weapon's alternate damage die.

When used one-handed:

use the normal damage die.

The system must record which mode was used for the specific attack.

Do not permanently modify the weapon's base damage die.

2024 Heavy property:

Attack has Disadvantage.

Do not convert this into an automatic attack failure.

Do not modify the attack bonus directly.

Apply it as an Advantage/Disadvantage source.

==================================================

When the Attack action is used and a Light weapon is used:

Do not create a second Bonus Action system.

Do not accidentally allow unlimited Light attacks.

Track the once-per-turn Light extra attack state correctly.

32. ATTACK ROLL
==================================================

Canonical formula:

attack_total =
selected_d20
+ ability_modifier
+ proficiency_bonus_if_proficient
+ authorized_attack_bonuses
+ authorized_attack_penalties

The target number is the target's authoritative AC after applicable
Cover and other target-side modifiers.

Hit if:

attack_total >= target_AC

Miss if:

attack_total < target_AC

Natural 20 and natural 1 override the normal comparison as specified
below.

==================================================
33. NATURAL 20
==================================================

If the selected attack d20 is 20:

- attack automatically hits
- target AC does not matter
- attack is a Critical Hit.

Do not allow a negative modifier or extreme AC to turn a natural 20
into a miss.

Do not reroll a natural 20.

==================================================
34. NATURAL 1
==================================================

If the selected attack d20 is 1:

- attack automatically misses
- target AC does not matter.

Do not let bonuses turn a natural 1 into a hit.

Do not convert natural 1 into a critical failure system beyond what the
2024 rules explicitly require.

Do not invent weapon breakage or self-damage.

==================================================
35. CRITICAL HIT
==================================================

On a Critical Hit:

roll all damage dice twice.

Examples:

1d8 -> 2d8
2d6 -> 4d6
1d8 + Sneak Attack 2d6
-> 2d8 + 4d6

Relevant modifiers are added normally, not doubled unless a specific
rule explicitly says otherwise.

Do not double:

- Strength modifier
- Dexterity modifier
- proficiency bonus
- static magic bonus
- unrelated fixed bonuses

unless an explicit rule says so.

All attack damage dice supplied through an authorized attack feature hook
must be correctly marked as critical-doublable.

39. RESISTANCE
==================================================

If target has Resistance to a damage type:

halve that damage.

Round down.

Multiple Resistance sources affecting the same damage type do not stack
beyond one Resistance.

Do not halve the attack roll.

Do not halve the damage dice before critical calculation.

==================================================
40. VULNERABILITY
==================================================

If target has Vulnerability to a damage type:

double that damage.

Apply Vulnerability after Resistance according to the 2024 damage

Multiple Vulnerability sources of the same type do not stack.

Do not double the attack roll.

Do not double the weapon's base damage dice before the critical rules.

==================================================
41. IMMUNITY
==================================================

If target is immune to a damage type:

final damage of that type is 0.

Do not remove the attack result itself.

An attack can still:

- hit
- critically hit
- trigger other authorized effects

even if a particular damage type deals no HP damage, unless the specific
rule says otherwise.

Do not globally suppress every effect merely because the target is
immune to the damage type.

==================================================
42. DAMAGE APPLICATION ORDER
==================================================

For each damage instance:

1. Roll base damage dice.
2. Add attacker's relevant damage modifier.
3. Add authorized bonuses/penalties.
4. Apply other explicit damage adjustments.
5. Apply Resistance.
6. Apply Vulnerability.
7. Apply Immunity.
8. Clamp final damage to minimum 0.
9. Apply to target HP.
10. Produce the canonical damage result.

Do not reorder these steps casually.

==================================================
43. ZERO DAMAGE
==================================================

An attack can hit while dealing 0 damage.

Examples:

- Resistance/Immunity
- Graze with a negative/zero modifier after minimum rules
- other explicit effects.

Do not convert:

hit + 0 damage

into:

miss.

Hit/miss and damage are separate outcomes.

==================================================
44. GRAZE MASTERY
==================================================

If the attacker has access to the weapon's Graze mastery and the attack
misses:

the weapon can deal damage equal to the ability modifier used for the
attack.

Damage type:

same as weapon.

The Graze damage cannot be increased except by increasing that ability
modifier.

Do not turn Graze into a normal weapon damage roll.

Do not roll the weapon's damage dice for Graze.

Do not apply the ability modifier twice.

If the ability modifier is negative, final damage cannot become negative.

==================================================
45. CLEAVE MASTERY
==================================================

If the attacker has access to Cleave and hits a creature with a melee
attack using that weapon:

the attacker may make an additional melee attack against a second
creature:

- within 5 feet of the first target
- also within the attacker's reach.

On hit:

the second target takes the weapon's damage.

Do not add the attacker's ability modifier to that damage unless the
modifier is negative.

Cleave can trigger only once per turn.

Cleave is an actual attack roll.

It must go through the same authoritative attack resolver.

Do not create a separate Cleave damage engine.

==================================================
46. PUSH MASTERY
==================================================

If the attacker has access to Push and hits:

target can be pushed up to 10 feet straight away from the attacker if
the target is Large or smaller.

Do not require a saving throw unless another rule modifies the effect.

Use authoritative position state.

Do not teleport the target.

Do not move through occupied/invalid spaces unless the existing movement
system explicitly allows the resulting movement.

If the existing position system cannot resolve the requested push:

do not invent a location.

Return a deterministic unresolved/blocked result.

==================================================
47. SAP MASTERY
==================================================

If the attacker has access to Sap and hits:

target has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the start of the
attacker's next turn.

Do not permanently impose Disadvantage.

Do not affect saving throws or ability checks.

Use the existing condition/effect duration system.

==================================================
48. SLOW MASTERY
==================================================

If the attacker has access to Slow and hits and deals damage:

target's Speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of the attacker's
next turn.

If the target is hit more than once by weapons with Slow:

the Speed reduction does not exceed 10 feet.

Do not stack:

-10
+
-10
=
-20

for Slow.

Use the existing temporary-effect system.

==================================================
49. TOPPLE MASTERY
==================================================

If the attacker has access to Topple and hits:

target makes a Constitution saving throw.

DC:

8
+
Proficiency Bonus
+
ability modifier used for the attack roll.

On failure:

target gains Prone.

On success:

no Prone from Topple.

Use the authoritative saving throw resolver.

Do not create a Topple-specific save engine.

==================================================
50. VEX MASTERY
==================================================

If the attacker has access to Vex and hits and deals damage:

the attacker gains Advantage on the next attack roll against that target
before the end of the attacker's next turn.

Do not grant permanent Advantage.

Do not grant Advantage against other targets.

Use the existing temporary-effect system.

==================================================

Do not hardcode:

Do not hardcode:

Provide the generic hook where missing.

==================================================
