DESIGN BRIEF — AI DUNGEON MASTER WEB EXPERIENCE

Design a premium dark-fantasy tabletop RPG interface for an AI-powered Dungeons & Dragons-style adventure.

The visual direction should evoke:

- Faerûn / Forgotten Realms high fantasy
- Sword Coast taverns, old libraries, guild halls and adventurer maps
- premium tabletop RPG books
- medieval illuminated manuscripts
- aged parchment maps
- carved dark wood
- worn stone
- forged iron
- antique brass
- candlelight
- ink sketches
- wax seals
- subtle arcane symbols

Reference mood / quality level:

- Baldur’s Gate 3 menus and character presentation
- classic Neverwinter Nights atmosphere
- premium D&D sourcebook layouts
- high-end fantasy tabletop character sheets
- old cartographer maps of the Sword Coast
- tasteful dark medieval fantasy interfaces

DO NOT directly copy logos, icons, artwork, layouts or proprietary assets from
D&D, Baldur’s Gate or other games.

The result should feel original but immediately recognizable as a serious
high-fantasy tabletop RPG experience.

==================================================
CORE EXPERIENCE
==================================================

This is NOT a dashboard SaaS app.

It is a living Dungeon Master interface.

The central focus must always be:

THE STORY.

The player should feel as if they are sitting at a fantasy table with a
Dungeon Master describing the world.

Natural-language input is the primary interaction.

Do not turn the game into a grid of buttons.

==================================================
DESKTOP LAYOUT
==================================================

Create a three-column desktop layout.

LEFT PANEL — CHARACTER

Approximately 20–23% width.

Include:

- character portrait frame
- character name
- race
- class
- level
- HP bar
- AC
- proficiency bonus
- STR / DEX / CON / INT / WIS / CHA
- inventory
- equipped weapon
- status effects

Visual treatment:

like an elegant fantasy character sheet mounted inside carved dark wood and
aged parchment.

HP should be visually prominent but refined.

Example:

CEDRIC
Elf Wizard · Level 1

HP
████████░░
8 / 10

AC 12

STR 11
DEX 15
CON 15
INT 10
WIS 14
CHA 14

Inventory
⚔ Kılıç
🎒 Macera Çantası

Do not make it look like a spreadsheet.

==================================================
CENTER PANEL — THE STORY
==================================================

Approximately 52–58% width.

This is the most important part of the entire interface.

Large atmospheric story area.

Dungeon Master narration should look like prose from a premium fantasy book,
not a Discord chat.

Example:

────────────────────────────────────

DUNGEON MASTER

Kapıyı araladığında tavernanın sıcaklığı yüzüne vurur.

Yağmurun sesi arkanda yavaşça kaybolurken içerideki şömine,
koyu renk ahşap masaların üzerine titrek bir ışık yayıyor.

Tezgâhın arkasında iri yapılı bir adam kupaları kuruluyor.

────────────────────────────────────

PLAYER

Barmene doğru gidip selam veriyorum.

────────────────────────────────────

DUNGEON MASTER

Adam elindeki kupayı tezgâha bırakıp sana bakıyor.

“Yolcuya benziyorsun,” diyor.
“İçki mi arıyorsun, yoksa bilgi mi?”

────────────────────────────────────

DM narration:
- larger serif typography
- comfortable line height
- subtle parchment/ink feeling
- highly readable

Player messages:
- visually distinct
- smaller
- elegant
- not giant chat bubbles

Avoid Messenger/Discord-style bubbles.

This should feel like interactive fantasy literature.

==================================================
PLAYER INPUT
==================================================

Persistent input area at the bottom of the story panel.

Large natural-language field:

"Ne yapmak istiyorsun?"

Example:

┌───────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Ne yapmak istiyorsun?                        │
│                                               │
│ > şöminenin yanındaki adama doğru gidiyorum  │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────┘

Add:

SEND button
optional subtle dice icon
optional microphone placeholder for future use

The textbox should invite free-form actions.

Do not show numbered action choices by default.

==================================================
RIGHT PANEL — WORLD / CONTEXT
==================================================

Approximately 22–25% width.

Show contextual information.

CURRENT LOCATION

Taverna

NPCs
- Kasım — Barmen
- Yaşlı Gezgin

Nearby
- Büyük Şömine
- Kemerli Pencere
- Arka Kapı

The panel should update depending on the situation.

Use tabs or elegant collapsible sections:

WORLD
NPCs
JOURNAL
COMBAT

Do not overload the screen.

==================================================
VISUAL LANGUAGE
==================================================

Color palette:

Background:
deep charcoal / nearly-black brown

Wood:
dark walnut

Parchment:
warm desaturated cream

Metal:
aged brass / bronze

Primary accent:
deep burgundy / oxblood

Secondary accent:
forest green

Magic accent:
very restrained muted arcane blue

Text:
warm ivory rather than pure white

Avoid:

- neon colors
- cyberpunk
- glassmorphism
- bright gradients
- generic modern SaaS cards
- excessive glowing borders
- mobile-game aesthetics
- cartoon fantasy
- World of Warcraft-style oversized ornaments

==================================================
MATERIALS
==================================================

Use subtle material cues:

- parchment grain
- leather
- dark carved wood
- scratched brass
- wrought iron
- stone
- faint map textures

Textures must remain restrained.

Readability comes first.

Do not cover the UI in noisy texture.

==================================================
ORNAMENTATION
==================================================

Use subtle fantasy decorations:

- corner engravings
- thin Celtic / medieval knotwork
- tiny runic separators
- compass rose
- map-line motifs
- miniature sword / shield / scroll icons
- wax seals
- heraldic dividers

Avoid enormous decorative frames that waste screen space.

==================================================
MAP MOTIF
==================================================

Use an old fantasy map aesthetic sparingly.

For example, behind the location panel:

faint parchment map lines
mountains
roads
forests
rivers
settlement dots

Very low opacity.

The map should feel like a cartographer’s map of Faerûn without reproducing an
official copyrighted map.

==================================================
TYPOGRAPHY
==================================================

Headings:

cinematic medieval / Roman-inscription serif.

Think:

Cinzel-like
Trajan-like
classic fantasy book title typography

Body narration:

high-quality readable literary serif.

UI labels:

small caps serif or restrained humanist sans-serif.

Avoid gimmicky unreadable medieval fonts for body text.

==================================================
MICROINTERACTIONS
==================================================

Keep animation subtle.

Examples:

- DM response softly fades in
- dice briefly rotate during a roll
- HP bar eases to its new value
- combat panel appears with restrained metal impact animation
- location name subtly reveals when entering a new area
- parchment panels gently illuminate on hover
- sending a player action gives a tiny ink/rune pulse

No excessive particle effects.

==================================================
LOADING / AI THINKING
==================================================

Do not use a generic spinner.

When the Dungeon Master is generating narration, show something atmospheric:

"The Dungeon Master düşünüyor..."

with:

- gently rotating d20
or
- quill writing animation
or
- faint glowing rune

Keep it elegant.

==================================================
RESPONSIVE BEHAVIOR
==================================================

Desktop is the primary target.

For narrower screens:

- story remains central
- character and world panels become slide-out drawers
- input remains fixed and easy to reach
- combat status remains accessible

Do not simply shrink the entire desktop interface.

==================================================
CHARACTER PORTRAITS
==================================================

Design a premium portrait frame suitable for later AI-generated portraits.

Portrait should feel like a painted fantasy character portrait.

Do not generate a specific character yet.

Create the placeholder/frame design only.

==================================================
DESIGN PRIORITIES
==================================================

Priority order:

1. atmosphere
2. readability
3. story immersion
4. clarity of game state
5. responsive behavior
6. subtle animation
7. decorative fantasy elements

Do not sacrifice readability for decoration.

==================================================
FINAL TARGET
==================================================

The player should open the page and immediately think:

"This feels like playing a premium digital D&D campaign in Faerûn."

Not:

"This looks like an AI chatbot with a fantasy skin."

The Dungeon Master narration must be the hero of the interface.
