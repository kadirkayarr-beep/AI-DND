========================================================

Do NOT model Neverwinter's primary canonical parent as:

Sword Coast Region

THE NORTH

and

SWORD COAST

as separate regional entries.

Neverwinter is covered under:

THE NORTH.

Therefore the initial production pack should conceptually be:

Forgotten Realms
→ The North
→ Neverwinter

western coast and nearby Sword Coast locations where sources support them.

Do not erase those relationships.

But the canonical region binding for this V1 must follow the current source.
