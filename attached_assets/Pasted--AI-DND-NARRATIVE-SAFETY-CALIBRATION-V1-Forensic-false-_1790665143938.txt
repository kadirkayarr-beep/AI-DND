==================================================
CURRENT VERIFIED PROBLEM
==================================================

The authenticated Phase-C/Phase-D live qwen3:8b smoke completed:

- 11 turns
- 21 model calls
- maximum 2 model calls per turn
- no reported canonical gameplay failures

However:

8 narrations were replaced by the narrator safety fallback.

The goal of this phase is NOT:

"disable safety so Qwen can say anything."

The goal is:

KEEP MECHANICAL SAFETY STRICT
WHILE REDUCING FALSE POSITIVES AGAINST HARMLESS STORYTELLING.

The game should be able to write vivid fiction without being allowed to
rewrite canonical game reality.

==================================================

==================================================

PYTHON DECIDES REALITY.

QWEN MAY DECORATE REALITY.

QWEN MAY NOT REWRITE REALITY.

Narrative safety must understand the difference.

==================================================
SAFETY TAXONOMY
==================================================

Create an explicit distinction between:

A. HARD CANONICAL VIOLATION
B. PLAYER-AGENCY VIOLATION
C. SOFT / HARMLESS NARRATIVE TEXTURE
D. AMBIGUOUS NARRATIVE CLAIM

==================================================
A. HARD CANONICAL VIOLATION
==================================================

Examples:

- changing HP
- inventing damage
- inventing healing
- changing inventory
- granting/removing gold
- changing quest status
- completing objectives
- creating spell effects
- awarding XP
- changing level
- changing combat target/result
- changing location canonically
- inventing canonical weather change
- resurrecting defeated entities
- moving canonical NPCs contrary to server state
- inventing important canonical items
- inventing important named NPCs as established reality

These should remain STRICTLY blocked or safely replaced.

==================================================
B. PLAYER-AGENCY VIOLATION
==================================================

Examples:

Qwen says:

"You draw your sword."

when the player did not.

"You agree to the bargain."

when the player did not.

"You tell Kasim your real name."

when the player did not.

"You decide he is lying."

without canonical support.

These should remain blocked or minimally repaired.

==================================================
C. HARMLESS NARRATIVE TEXTURE
==================================================

Examples:

- a floorboard creaks
- candlelight flickers
- rain taps against glass
- smoke curls above the hearth
- a distant patron laughs
- Kasim wipes a glass
- boots leave wet marks
- a chair scrapes somewhere behind the player
- wind carries the smell of pine
- an NPC raises an eyebrow
- a room feels warm
- distant bells ring

These are generally NOT canonical game-state mutations.

They should normally be allowed.

Narrative safety must not treat every descriptive noun or verb as a new
canonical world object.

==================================================
D. AMBIGUOUS NARRATIVE CLAIM
==================================================

Examples:

"Kasim seems nervous."

"The alley looks recently disturbed."

"The barkeep glances toward the cellar."

These MAY be harmless presentation,
but they can sometimes imply information.

Define a safe policy.

Prefer:

allow subjective/perceptual texture

unless it establishes a mechanical or secret fact as objectively true.

Example:

Allowed:

"Kasim's fingers tighten briefly around the glass."

Not automatically allowed as fact:

"Kasim is secretly a Zhentarim spy."

==================================================
CANONICAL ASSERTION VS LITERARY DESCRIPTION
==================================================

Implement or improve a distinction between:

CANONICAL ASSERTION

and:

LITERARY DESCRIPTION.

For example:

"The rain begins."

could imply a canonical weather change.

But:

"Rain patters against the windows."

is harmless if canonical weather is already rain.

Similarly:

"A guard named Halric approaches you and offers a quest."

introduces significant canonical entities/state.

But:

"A few anonymous guards pass at the far end of the street."

can be harmless background texture.

Safety needs context.

==================================================
KNOWN ENTITY HANDLING
==================================================

Do not loosen identity rules.

Stable IDs remain authoritative.

A safety calibration must NOT cause:

- two same-name NPCs to merge
- unknown NPC memory to become canonical
- procedural background figures to become persistent entities
- canonical NPC location to change because narration said so

Narrative texture can mention anonymous background people,
but cannot silently promote them into canonical entities.

==================================================
NPC GESTURES AND EXPRESSIONS
==================================================

A recurring NPC must be allowed to:

- smile
- frown
- look away
- raise an eyebrow
- sigh
- pause
- speak softly
- wipe a glass
- fold their arms

without every gesture being interpreted as canonical state mutation.

These gestures are narrative continuity unless the engine explicitly stores
them as mechanics.

==================================================
PLAYER SENSORY EXPERIENCE
==================================================

Narrator may safely describe:

- what the player sees
- hears
- smells
- feels physically from already-canonical conditions

But may not decide:

- what the player believes
- what the player chooses
- who the player trusts
- what moral conclusion the player reaches

==================================================
SAFETY SHOULD BE MINIMAL
==================================================

When a response contains one unsafe clause and several safe paragraphs,
avoid replacing the entire narration if a narrower deterministic repair is
possible.

Investigate a tiered safety model.

Possible conceptual levels:

LEVEL 0 — ACCEPT
No violation.

LEVEL 1 — ACCEPT WITH SAFE NORMALIZATION
Minor harmless wording issue.

LEVEL 2 — REMOVE / REWRITE ONLY UNSAFE CLAIM
If a deterministic safe repair can preserve the rest.

LEVEL 3 — FULL FALLBACK
Use only for serious or structurally unsafe output.

Do NOT implement unsafe free-form rewriting if it requires unbounded extra
model calls.

Prefer deterministic or tightly bounded repairs.

==================================================
NO HIDDEN THIRD MODEL CALL
==================================================

Current maximum:

2 model calls per turn.

Preserve it.

Do not solve false positives by adding:

generate
→ safety reject
→ regenerate
→ safety reject
→ regenerate again

No hidden third call.

If a rewrite strategy uses Qwen,
it must remain inside the existing 2-call ceiling.

Prefer prevention and deterministic post-processing over retries.

==================================================
CONTEXT-AWARE SAFETY
==================================================

Safety validation must receive enough authoritative context to distinguish
contradiction from valid prose.

Relevant context may include:

- current location
- environment
- time_of_day
- weather
- present NPC IDs
- current canonical target
- combat state
- inventory
- quest state
- skill result
- current action
- narrative memory
- known scene type

Do not compare narration against an incomplete snapshot and then punish
correct prose.

==================================================
SCENE-AWARE SAFETY
==================================================

Different scene types permit different harmless texture.

EXPLORATION:
more environment description

DIALOGUE:
more gestures, expressions, pauses, vocal tone

COMBAT:
more motion/impact imagery, strictly bound to structured outcome

AFTERMATH:
more emotional/environmental description

DIRECT QUERY:
very little decorative prose

Do not apply identical literary expectations to every scene.

==================================================
COMBAT SAFETY
==================================================

Combat remains especially strict.

Canonical structured combat result owns:

- actor
- target
- roll
- hit/miss
- damage
- HP
- status
- current turn
- initiative
- round

Qwen may dramatize the result.

Example:

Canonical:
MISS

Allowed:

"The blade whistles past the goblin's shoulder and bites into empty air."

Not allowed:

"The blade cuts deeply into the goblin."

==================================================
SKILL CHECK SAFETY
==================================================

Structured skill_check_result remains authority.

Canonical:

FAILURE

Allowed:

"The tracks blur into the churned mud until you can no longer distinguish
their direction."

Not allowed:

"You identify the exact path the bandits took."

unless Python outcome supports it.

==================================================
QUEST SAFETY
==================================================

Quest Outcomes V1 is now canonical.

Narrator may discuss an ACTIVE quest.

Narrator may NOT:

- create quest
- accept quest
- progress objective
- complete quest
- cancel quest

unless canonical Python state already contains that transition.

==================================================
NARRATIVE MEMORY SAFETY
==================================================

Narrative memory is context, not canonical mechanics.

Safety must distinguish:

"we previously discussed the caravan"

from:

"the caravan quest is complete."

Memory callbacks should not trigger false positives simply because they refer
to old events.
