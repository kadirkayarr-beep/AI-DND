SOURCE AUTHORITY
============================================================

Mechanical authority:

The following PHB 2024 baseline has already been source-verified and may be
used for this pass.

Chapter 7, approximately printed pp. 234–238:

- A spell must be available/prepared through a legitimate feature or other
  supported source before it can be cast.
- Cantrips are level 0 and do not consume spell slots.
- Level 1+ spells normally consume an appropriate spell slot.
- A spell may use a slot of its level or higher where supported.
- Most spells use the Magic action; spell descriptions may instead specify
  Bonus Action, Reaction, or longer casting times.
- A creature can expend only one spell slot to cast a spell on a turn.
- Range and target restrictions come from the spell.
- Required components must be available for a legal cast.
- A spell target normally requires a clear path and cannot be behind Total
  Cover.
- An actually invalid target can still consume the slot under the PHB rule;
  this is different from the ENGINE not supporting a requested spell mode.
- Spell save DC:
  8 + spellcasting ability modifier + Proficiency Bonus.
- Spell attack modifier:
  spellcasting ability modifier + Proficiency Bonus.
- Spell effects are exactly the supported effects defined by the spell.

Rules Glossary, Concentration:

- only the supported concentration owner may decide concentration state;
- starting another concentration effect ends the previous concentration;
- damage requires the appropriate Constitution save;
- DC is 10 or half damage taken rounded down, whichever is higher, capped at
  30;
- Incapacitated or death ends Concentration.

IMPORTANT:

Concentration is a verified rule, but it is NOT automatically in the bounded
Spells V1 implementation scope below.

Do not implement it merely because the rule exists.
